Architecture

Every clause,
decoded.

ConsentLedger turns dense legal fine print into structured facts that you can check against the text.

Named Entity Recognition
Legal-BERT, fine-tuned on privacy policies and then on AA documents, marks the data a policy collects, the purposes it states and the third parties it names.
Faithfulness Gate
Each machine-written summary is checked against its passage. A summary that reverses a meaning, names the wrong party or invents a name is withheld, and the facts are shown instead.
Cryptographic Fingerprint
A Keccak-256 fingerprint binds the policy's facts to the consent artefact. Recorded on the Sepolia test network, it lets anyone check later that neither was changed.
ReBIT Consent Parser
Reads the ReBIT consent artefact: who receives the data, why, for how long, how often it may be fetched and how long it may be kept.
Denial & Covenant Detection
Marks what a policy promises not to do, such as never accessing your login credentials or never selling your number, apart from what it does collect.
Text, HTML and PDF
Accepts plain text, HTML and PDF. Documents are cleaned and split into passages before analysis.
