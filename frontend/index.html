Architecture

Every clause,
decoded.

ConsentLedger decomposes dense legal fine print into structured, mathematically auditable facts — with zero ambiguity.

Named Entity Recognition
Legal-BERT identifies and classifies every collected data attribute, authorized purpose, and external recipient with token-level span resolution.
Faithfulness Gate
Every AI-generated synthesis is cross-verified against source passages. Hallucinations are actively discarded in favour of verbatim legal facts.
Cryptographic Fingerprint
A canonical Keccak-256 state commitment binds the policy and consent artefact together, verifiable on the Sepolia Ethereum testnet.
ReBIT Consent Parser
Parses machine-readable Account Aggregator schemas: evaluating retention windows, fetch periodicity, and data consumer identity boundaries.
Denial & Covenant Detection
Distinguishes negative commitments such as covenants prohibiting credential harvesting or resale of identifiers from actual collection practices.
Multi-Format Normalization
Direct ingestion of plain text, HTML, and structured document formats with deterministic whitespace normalization before analysis.
